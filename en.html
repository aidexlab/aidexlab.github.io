02 — Lingoop Bites
Lingoop Bites korean

Korean, one bite
at a time.

The second Korean app from the team behind Lingoop — not for exam prep, but for anyone who got curious about Korean through a drama, a song, or one trip to Seoul.

Open it and today's six cards are already on the table, in pictures. No login, no deck to pick — just take a bite. Two minutes clears the plate; still hungry? One more serving.

Pictures before letters
20 decks — greetings, side dishes, the subway, K-drama — all free. Picture quizzes and flashcards show you the meaning first; the word follows.
Boxes that drift away as you learn
Words you get right move up a box and come back after 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days — served once more, right when you'd forget.
Sound always travels with you
Every word has Korean audio. On the subway or on a plane, it all works without internet.
