Lingoop Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Made by AiDEX LAB · 한국어

AiDEX LAB ("we") operates the Lingoop app (the "App"). This policy explains how we handle your personal information, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea.

1. What we collect

WhenWhat
Sign up / log inEmail and name (from your Google account if you use Google Sign-In). If you sign up with email, your password is stored encrypted by Google Firebase — we cannot see it.
ProfileNationality, age group, visa/stay status, preferred job category, native language, expected entry month
Recorded automatically (profile)Actual entry date — the day you first open the App while connected to a Korean mobile network (if you were already in Korea, this may differ from your real entry date). We use only the network's country code — never your GPS location. Residence stage (calculated from your entry dates), avatar type (set by your visa), and admin data such as onboarding status and save times
While you studyXP, learning progress, bookmarks, review words, daily study records (last 400 days), total study time, longest streak. If you are logged in, these are saved to our server so you can continue on a new device.
Automatically while using the AppUsage events (screens and features used, scores), app language, device type, OS and app version, crash logs, device identifiers (advertising ID, app installation ID, etc.), how the App was installed, and approximate region (country/city level) estimated from your IP address. This data is not directly linked to your account.
When you buyPayments are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. The App stores the items you own, purchase time, and check time on your device and does not send them to our server. (If Google backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may back up app data to your own Google account.) Purchase events (item, price, currency) may be included in usage statistics.
When you send an error reportReport type, description, photos you attach (location data removed before sending), device info (maker, model, Android version), the screen you reported from, app language, time of report, and a random number created per device (not linked to your account). We do not send your email or name.
When you email usYour email address and message
When you ask for a launch notice (only in app version 1.1.5 and earlier)Your device's registration for the notice is recorded with Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Whether you asked is stored on your device; we do not store it on our server.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the service (log-in, saving and restoring your study records)
  • To show your profile
  • To handle your questions and error reports
  • To improve the App through usage statistics and crash analysis
  • To confirm purchases
  • To send launch notices (app version 1.1.5 and earlier) — only to people who asked for them
  • For service development and business analysis, using statistics that cannot identify you

3. How long we keep it

DataRetention
Profile and study recordsDeleted when you leave the service. If you are also logged in on another device, please log out there too.
Login account (email, name)Shared with our other app, Lingoop Visa, so leaving Lingoop does not delete it. To delete your whole account, email us (section 9). We delete it without delay.
Launch-notice requests (app version 1.1.5 and earlier)Not kept on our server (earlier records were all deleted on October 6, 2026). When you turn off the notice on the request screen, your device's registration for it is removed as well.
Error reports (content, photos, email copy)3 years after resolution
Email inquiries3 years after resolution
Usage and crash dataFollows Google's retention settings (Google Analytics: event data 2 months, user data 14 months)

When the retention period ends, we delete the data without delay so it cannot be recovered.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or give your personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Your rights

  • View / correct: you can edit your name, nationality, age group, visa, native language, and expected entry month on your profile screen. Email and preferred job category can be viewed only. Actual entry date and residence stage are not shown in the App — email us to view or correct them.
  • Delete (leave the service): Profile screen → Delete Account, or email us
  • Stop processing: email us
  • Turn off notifications: study reminders in Settings; launch notices (app version 1.1.5 and earlier) on the request screen
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's settings

We act on your request without delay and let you know the result.

7. Children under 14

The App is not intended for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If we learn we have collected it without a guardian's consent, we delete it immediately.

8. How we protect your data

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  • Passwords are stored encrypted by Google Firebase (we cannot see them)
  • Location data is removed from attached photos
  • Access to personal data is limited to the minimum people and permissions needed
